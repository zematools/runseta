Runseta Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Runseta is developed by Amanuel Yohannes. This policy describes how the Runseta Windows app handles your information.

Your checklist data

Runseta stores your checklists, run notes, progress, history, and preferences locally on your device. This content may contain personal information you choose to enter. Runseta does not automatically send your checklist content to the developer and does not provide its own cloud synchronization.

Backups, exports, and sharing

When you import, export, copy a summary, or print, the selected content is handled through the destination you choose. Exported files, clipboard content, and printed or saved PDFs may be accessible to other software or people with access to those destinations. Windows or other backup services may back up local files according to your settings.

Pro purchases and license recovery

Pro checkout takes place in your browser through Dodo Payments. Dodo processes the contact, billing, and payment information you provide, handles the order, and delivers your license key. The Runseta app does not collect your payment card details. The developer can access customer and order information needed to support purchases. Find my license opens Dodo's customer portal, where you can request access using your purchase email.

License verification

Runseta connects to the developer's licensing service, hosted on Cloudflare, to create checkout sessions, check payment status, activate Pro, and periodically verify licenses. Requests use an installation identifier, checkout identifiers, and a license key or signed license token as needed. The service stores checkout and activation records, references to Dodo customer, payment, and entitlement records, and license status to enforce the device allowance and handle refunds or revocations. Your checklist content is not included in these licensing requests.

The service processes your IP address for request rate limiting and stores keyed hashes for its rate-limit records. Cloudflare may also process network and request information to operate and protect the service. Activation and purchase-reference records are retained to maintain license access and prevent misuse; uninstalling Runseta does not automatically delete these server records. Contact support with questions or requests about your licensing information.

Advertising and diagnostics

Runseta does not include advertising or developer-operated usage tracking. Windows, Microsoft Store, and WebView2 may process service or diagnostic information under their own settings and privacy policies.

Contacting support

If you email serviceamanuel@gmail.com, the developer receives the information and attachments you send and uses them to respond and troubleshoot. Email providers also process those messages under their policies. Please avoid including sensitive information that is not needed for support.

Your choices

You can edit or delete content in the app and export a backup. Deleting content from Runseta does not delete copies in exported files, the clipboard, or external backups. You may request deletion of support correspondence by email, subject to any necessary retention obligations.

This website

This product page does not include developer-added analytics, advertising, or account registration. Its hosting provider may process technical request information to deliver and protect the website. Downloads are hosted on GitHub Releases. GitHub, Microsoft, Dodo Payments, and Cloudflare operate under their own privacy policies.

Changes and contact

Updates to this policy will be reflected in the date above. For questions or requests, contact serviceamanuel@gmail.com.
