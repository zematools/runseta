Good to know

A few questions,
answered.

Can I use Runseta offline?

Yes. Your checklist work is stored locally and works offline. After activation, Pro works offline without a time limit. Internet is required for downloads, purchases, and activation. License status is checked when online.

Where does my data go?

Your checklists, notes, and run history stay on your device. Runseta does not automatically upload that content to a developer server and does not have its own cloud sync. See the privacy policy for details.

What happens if I leave a run unfinished?

Your progress is saved. Come back and resume the run when you are ready. A run can only be completed after every step is checked.

Can I back up my checklists?

Yes. Backup and restore are available in both Free and Pro. Keep your exported backup somewhere safe if you want to restore your data later.

How do I unlock Pro?

Select Get Pro inside Runseta and complete secure checkout with Dodo Payments in your browser. Return to Runseta so it can check payment and activate Pro. Your license key is also sent by email; you can enter it using Activate Pro.

Can I download it now?

Yes. Download Runseta for Windows x64 and run the installer. The Microsoft Store version is coming soon.
